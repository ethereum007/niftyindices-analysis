What We Do

Affle is a global technology company with a proprietary consumer intelligence platform that transforms ads into recommendations helping marketers to effectively identify, engage, acquire and drive transactions with their potential and existing users. Affle 3i Limited successfully completed its IPO in India on 08.08.19 and now trades on the stock exchanges (BSE: 542752 & NSE: AFFLE)

Corporate & Product Milestones

2005 - 2010
Early beginnings & foundations

Company founded, launch of SMS 2.0, early teams in Singapore, India & UK

2011 - 2015
Platform expansion & diversification

Launch of ad2c, Ripple & mTraction with focus on building tech stack and laying foundation for scale

2016 - 2019
Business strengthening and public listing (IPO)

Expanding product & platform tech with acquisition of Vizury, Shoffr & RevX. Successful launch of IPO

2020 - 2024
Global scale, tech recognition & acquisitions

Listed on BSE & NSE, global expansion with the acquisition of Appnext & mediasmart, followed by Jampp & Youappi. DPTM certification from IMDA Singapore, and patents grant in US & India

2025
Growth acceleration and 10x vision

Positioning for next-gen growth, with focus on innovation, impact & intelligence. Strengthening privacy and compliance credentials with ISO certification and re-accreditation by IMDA Singapore.

2026
Strategic acquisition strengthening Affle 3i's tech stack

Acquired AdColony's technology assets and trademark from Digital Turbine, expanding our global reach and Consumer Intelligence.
