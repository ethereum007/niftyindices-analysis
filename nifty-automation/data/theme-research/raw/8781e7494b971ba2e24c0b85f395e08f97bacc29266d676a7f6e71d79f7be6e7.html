            Things to know

            How to apply

            You can open an AU Savings Account through any of the following options:
             

            Video Banking
            Open your account online through Video Banking Simply keep your PAN and Aadhaar handy. Click here AU Video Banking to get started.
             

            Visit a Branch
            Visit your nearest AU branch with your KYC documents and passport-sized photographs. Our team will assist you with the account opening process.
             

            Call Customer Care
            Call us at 1800 1200 1200 for assistance with opening an AU Savings Account.

            Eligibility

            To apply, you must:

            • Be a citizen of India
            • Be 18 years of age or above
            • Have the necessary KYC documents issued by the Government of India.

            Fees & charges

            We believe in complete transparency and provide you with a detailed list of charges applicable to the services we offer. Depending on the type of Savings Account you hold, these may include charges related to your debit card, payment and collection services, transactions, and delivery fees. For more details, please refer to our Service Fee Reckoner.

            Documents required

            You need to have the following documents to open an AU Digital Savings Account:

            • Aadhaar Card
            • Pan Card

            Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

            What is an AU Digital Savings Account?

            A digital savings bank account is an instant account that you can open online in minutes from any place convenient for you. The process is entirely paperless and is done using our video banking facility.

            What are the interest rates on opening a Digital Savings Account?

            We offer attractive interest rates to our customers. Click here for more information on interest rates.

            What are the different steps involved in opening a Digital Savings Account?

            To open a Digital Savings Account, you need to complete the entire process on our Video Banking platform. For this process you need to:

            • Start by filling up your details online.
            • After you have filled in all the required details, our bank agent will carry out basic checks through a video interaction.
            • The agent will guide you through the process. Just keep your original PAN card, a blank paper and a blue/black pen handy.

            Is aadhaar required for video KYC?

            Yes. Aadhaar number is required for video KYC. Your Aadhaar number will be verified through an OTP received on your registered mobile number. You don’t need to carry your physical Aadhaar Card during the Video KYC process.
