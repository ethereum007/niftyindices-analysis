FAQs on Paints & Texture
1. How can I choose the right paint for my home?

Consider the room’s purpose, lighting, and size. Use lighter shades to make spaces feel larger and darker for warmth. Choose washable paints in high-traffic areas for easy maintenance.

 
2. How can I choose the right texture for my home?

Texture depends on your style and room function. Smooth textures suit modern designs, while rough finishes add depth. Test samples on walls to see how they complement your space.

 
3. How do I calculate the amount of paint needed for a room?

Measure wall height and width to find total area. Subtract windows and doors. Divide by the paint's coverage per gallon (on label). Always buy extra for touch-ups.

 
4. Where can I get painters to paint my home?

You can find painters via local listings, online platforms, or recommendations from friends. Check reviews, licenses, and previous work before hiring.

 
5. What is the durability of textured paints?

Textured paints are durable and mask imperfections but may chip over time in high-traffic areas. Use good-quality paints and consider sealing to extend their lifespan.

 
6. How do I maintain textured walls?

Dust them regularly with a soft cloth or vacuum. For stains, lightly wipe with a damp sponge and mild cleaner. Avoid harsh scrubbing to preserve the texture's integrity.
