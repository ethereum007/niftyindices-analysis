Escorts Kubota's Export Division brings you two distinct brands, Farmtrac and Powertrac, designed to cater to the diverse needs of farmers worldwide. Whether you're seeking premium performance or economical efficiency, our tractors are engineered to excel in all conditions. With Farmtrac tractors offering power from 20 to 120 HP and Powertrac tractors ranging from 20 to 90 HP, we ensure a solution for every farm. Our products are proudly exported to over 62 countries, with a presence across all continents. Supported by advanced manufacturing facilities in both Asia and Europe, we deliver cutting-edge technology to our customers. Alongside our tractors, Farmpower implements offer a comprehensive range tailored to diverse soil types and farming conditions, helping farmers achieve maximum productivity.
