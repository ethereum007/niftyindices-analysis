Do's and don'ts of taking a personal loan

Compare personal loan offers carefully to understand the repayment commitment, total borrowing cost and eligibility requirements before applying.

Don'ts

Compare EMIs: Calculate your monthly EMI and ensure it fits comfortably within your budget after essential expenses.

Don't overlook affordability: Avoid choosing a loan with an EMI that strains your monthly finances.

Don't focus only on low EMIs: Longer tenures may reduce monthly payments but increase the overall interest cost.

Things to know before applying for a personal loan

Before applying for a personal loan, review the eligibility criteria, interest rates, repayment tenure and applicable charges to understand the borrowing costs and choose a repayment plan that suits your budget.
 

  • Eligibility requirements: Before applying for a personal loan, check the minimum age, income, credit score and employment criteria. Meeting the eligibility requirements helps you understand whether you may qualify, although final approval depends on verification and the applicable terms and conditions.
  • Interest rate: Compare personal loan interest rates before choosing an offer. Your rate may depend on your credit profile, income and other assessment factors. Even a small difference in the interest rate can affect your monthly EMI and total repayment amount.
  • Repayment tenure: Choose a repayment tenure that fits your monthly budget and financial commitments. A longer tenure generally reduces your EMI but may increase the total interest payable. A shorter tenure can reduce interest costs but requires higher monthly repayments.
  • Fees and charges: Review the processing fee, applicable taxes and other charges before accepting a personal loan offer. These costs affect the total borrowing expense beyond the interest rate. Read the fee schedule and loan agreement to understand the charges applicable to your loan.
  • Prepayment and foreclosure: Check the terms for part-prepayment and foreclosure before taking a personal loan. Charges and conditions vary by loan type and agreement. Understanding these rules helps you assess whether early repayment is suitable for your finances and could reduce future interest costs.

Comparing these factors beforehand can help you make an informed borrowing decision and manage your personal loan repayments more effectively.

Tips for using an instant personal loan smartly

Using an instant personal loan wisely starts with borrowing only what you need, planning repayments around your income and understanding the loan terms. These practical tips can help you manage borrowing costs and maintain repayment discipline.
 

  • Borrow only what you need
    Estimate your expenses before choosing an instant personal loan amount. Borrowing only what you require can keep your EMI manageable and reduce the total interest payable over the repayment tenure.
  • Plan your EMI around income
    Calculate your monthly EMI against your take-home income and essential expenses. Keeping repayments within your budget helps you meet instalment deadlines without compromising routine financial commitments or savings.
  • Compare interest rates and charges
    Review interest rates, processing fees and applicable charges across suitable personal loan offers. Comparing the total borrowing cost helps you understand your repayment obligation and choose an option that fits your finances.
  • Choose a suitable repayment tenure
    Select a repayment tenure based on your monthly budget and financial goals. A longer tenure may lower your EMI but increase total interest, while a shorter tenure usually requires higher monthly payments.
  • Keep funds for emergencies
    Maintain a separate emergency reserve from your regular savings wherever possible. Having funds available for unexpected expenses can help you manage financial setbacks without relying on additional borrowing while repaying your personal loan.
  • Pay your EMIs on time
    Set reminders or use an appropriate payment facility to avoid missing EMI due dates. Timely repayments help you maintain a positive repayment history and reduce the risk of late-payment charges, subject to your loan terms.

How is personal loan interest calculated?

You can calculate personal loan interest and the monthly EMI using the standard formula:

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)ⁿ / [(1 + R)ⁿ – 1]

Here, P represents the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the repayment tenure in months. You can also use the personal loan EMI calculator to quickly calculate the estimated EMI and total interest payable.

How does personal loan balance transfer work?

A personal loan balance transfer allows you to move your existing outstanding loan to another lender, usually to access better terms or a more suitable interest rate. The new lender settles the outstanding amount with your existing lender, after which you repay the transferred loan under the new agreement.

Before choosing a balance transfer, compare the new interest rate, processing fees, foreclosure or other applicable charges, repayment tenure and overall cost. A lower interest rate does not always mean lower total borrowing costs. Check the terms carefully and assess whether the transfer suits your repayment needs.

Does a personal loan offer any tax benefits?

A personal loan does not automatically qualify for tax benefits. Interest may be deductible when the borrowed funds are used for eligible business expenses or qualifying house-property purposes, subject to applicable tax laws and conditions.

Why was my personal loan application rejected and what should I do?

A few common reasons for personal loan rejection include:

  • CIBIL Score below 650

  • High FOIR (as per lender criteria)

  • Being on probation at a new job

  • Document mismatched

  • Multiple recent loan applications

  • Past loan settlements or write-offs

Rejection does not affect your CIBIL Score but repeated enquiries can. Identify the issue, fix it and then reapply.

What factors affect my personal loan interest rate?

The following factors determine the interest rate offered on your personal loan:

(1) CIBIL score — a score of 650 or above typically qualifies for the lowest rates.

(2) Monthly income — higher income signals lower repayment risk.

(3) Employer profile — employees of listed companies or government employees may be eligible for interest rate offers based on the lender’s assessment.

(4) Loan amount and tenure — larger amounts or longer tenures may influence the interest rate offered, depending on the lender’s policy.

(5) Existing relationship — existing Bajaj Finance customers may receive interest rate offers based on the lender’s assessment and prevailing policies.

(6) Debt‑to‑income ratio — a lower existing obligation profile improves your rate eligibility.
