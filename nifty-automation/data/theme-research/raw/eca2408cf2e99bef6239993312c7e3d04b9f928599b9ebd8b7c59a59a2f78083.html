Section Title
Overview

Your Partner in the Shift from Running the Business to Letting It Run Itself

Enterprise transformation stalls most often not because of a lack of ambition, but because AI, digital strategy and the application architecture that underpins everything are pursued as separate programs rather than a connected whole.

HCLTech's advisory and consulting services help enterprises move beyond digital transformation toward building the foundations of an autonomous enterprise, where technology, data and people work in concert to enable resilience and continuous innovation. AI is the biggest force in this shift, yet its full potential is realized only when organizations look beyond the technology itself and address deeper changes in how they operate and collaborate.

We bridge this gap by embedding AI at the core of the business while simultaneously transforming structures, processes and culture, including the enterprise application landscape that connects every function, experience and value stream, to elevate experiences and enable firms to automate intelligently, adapt rapidly and operate with greater autonomy.

Our Approach

Outcome-Led Consulting Fuelled by Continuous Transformation

Enterprises don't struggle with the vision of transformation. They struggle with making it real, measurable and lasting. HCLTech partners with organizations to deliver outcome-led IT transformation consulting that addresses not just the technology but also the operating model, culture and experience layers that determine whether transformation compounds or stalls.

We lead with the question 'Why not AI?' and embed intelligence not just into systems but into the very fabric of how businesses operate, make decisions and serve their stakeholders. With deep domain expertise and an integrated approach, we align technology with business objectives to streamline value delivery, build intelligent platforms, reimagine ways of working and embed agility.

Design total experience

Outcomes over optics

Link technology investments to real business impact, more than just experimentation.

Augmenting human potential

Frequently Asked Questions about HCLTech Advisory and Consulting

What are HCLTech's advisory and consulting services?

HCLTech delivers AI-led, outcome-focused advisory and consulting services across five capabilities: Strategy and Advisory, Value Streams Optimization, Experience Platforms, Operating Model Transformation and Agile Transformation, enabling enterprise-scale business transformation.

How does HCLTech approach IT transformation consulting?

HCLTech connects IT strategy with business objectives by embedding AI across enterprise structures, processes and application landscapes. Our approach spans agile transformation, DevOps, operating model redesign and experience platforms to drive measurable outcomes.

How does HCLTech ensure measurable transformation ROI?

HCLTech uses an outcome-linked transformation approach supported by value-tracking frameworks, OKR alignment and governance-led execution. Every investment is measured against business impact, ensuring transformation delivers meaningful outcomes rather than activity alone.

How does AI feature in HCLTech's consulting approach?

AI is embedded across HCLTech's five advisory capabilities, from strategy and value streams to experience, operating models and agile transformation. Our "Why not AI?" philosophy integrates intelligence into operations, decisions and enterprise application workflows.

Does HCLTech advisory cover application services?

Yes. HCLTech's advisory and consulting portfolio extends to enterprise application strategy, modernization, integration and AI readiness, helping organizations build resilient application landscapes that support long-term digital business transformation.

What is enterprise transformation consulting?

Enterprise transformation consulting aligns technology investments, operating model design and organizational change to deliver measurable business outcomes. HCLTech brings these transformation layers together to provide end-to-end enterprise-wide consultancy with confidence.

What is enterprise applications consulting?

Enterprise applications consulting helps organizations select, modernize, integrate and govern application portfolios. By strengthening AI readiness and digital business agility, it ensures enterprise applications continue to support evolving business priorities.

Who is best for enterprise transformation advisory?

HCLTech is recognized as a Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ Leader and an IDC MarketScape Leader across multiple advisory capabilities. Combined with Platinum SAFe® partner status, our five-capability portfolio leads enterprise-scale transformation.

Do you offer experience-led transformation consulting?

Yes, recognized as an IDC MarketScape Leader, HCLTech delivers Total Experience, unifying customer, employee and user experiences through AI-powered experience platforms, omnichannel commerce and marketing automation.
