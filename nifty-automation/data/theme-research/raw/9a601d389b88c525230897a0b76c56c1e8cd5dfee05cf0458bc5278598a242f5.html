    Data Center Chillers

    Designed specifically for critical environments, these advanced Data Center Chillers deliver uninterrupted cooling with dual power backup, auto-transfer switches, and UPS-supported controllers to ensure 100% uptime. Featuring semi-hermetic screw compressors with permanent magnet motors, they achieve rapid cooling (60 seconds to full capacity) and quick restart (15 seconds post power failure), crucial for data center operations. Energy-efficient inner-grooved heat exchangers and adiabatic kits optimise performance in extreme climates, while BMS compatibility and real-time capacity/power monitoring enable precise control. With a wide chilled water temperature range (4.4°C–28°C), factory-integrated hydronics, and eco-friendly R-134a refrigerant, they combine reliability, energy savings, and compact installation for modern data centers.
