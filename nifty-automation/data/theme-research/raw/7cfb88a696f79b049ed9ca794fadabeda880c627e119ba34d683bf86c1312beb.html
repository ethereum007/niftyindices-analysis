Company Profile

Quick Summary:

Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL) is owned by the Government of India. Established in 1967, it is the only government company in India that mines copper ore and produces pure refined copper across 5 different states.

Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL), is a Schedule ‘A’ Mini-Ratna, Category-I Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE) under the administrative control of the Ministry of Mines. Incorporated in 1967 under the Indian Companies Act, 1956, HCL is the only company in India engaged in copper ore mining and holds all the operating mining leases for copper ore in the country.

The company's current focus lies in exploration, mining, and beneficiation of copper ore to produce and sell copper concentrate. HCL operates copper mines at Malanjkhand (Madhya Pradesh), Khetri (Rajasthan) and Ghatsila (Jharkhand). It has facilities of a primary smelter and refinery at Ghatsila (Jharkhand), secondary smelter and refining facilities at Jhagadia (Gujarat), and a Continuous Cast Copper Wire Rod plant at Taloja (Maharashtra). Operations at the Jhagadia and Ghatsila smelting/refining facilities have been suspended since 2019 due to business considerations. The operations at the Taloja plant are currently limited to third-party tolling activities.

As of April 1, 2026, the company employed 1,154 regular personnel, comprising 552 executives and 602 non-executives. The Registered and Corporate Office of HCL is located at Tamra Bhavan, 1, Ashutosh Chowdhury Avenue, Kolkata, West Bengal, Pin -700019

As of March 31, 2026, the authorized capital of the company stood at ₹1,100 crore, with a paid-up capital of ₹483.51 crore. The Government of India holds a 66.14% equity stake in the company.

In the concluded financial year FY 2025-26, the company has earned a Profit Before Tax (PBT) of Rs. 1232.73 Crore against a Net Sales turnover of Rs. 3054.4 Crore. For 06 months ending Sept’2025 the Company achieved a PBT of Rs. 427.99 Crore against a Net Sales turnover of Rs.1216.19 Crore. The Company has been consistently distributing dividends to its shareholders since FY 2020–21.

Key Financial Highlights (FY 2025–26):
  • Sales Turnover: Rs 3,054.40 crore
  • Net Profit (PAT): Rs 1,232.73 crore
Additional Financial Highlights (Six Months Ended September 2025):
  • Sales Turnover: Rs 1,216.19 crore
  • Net Profit (PAT): Rs 427.99 crore

Operating Units across India:

  • Malanjkhand Copper Project (MCP) at Malanjkhand, Madhya Pradesh
  • Khetri Copper Complex (KCC) at Khetrinagar, Rajasthan
  • Indian Copper Complex (ICC) at Ghatsila, Jharkhand
  • Taloja Copper Project (TCP) at Taloja, Maharashtra
  • Gujarat Copper Project (GCP) at Jhagadia, Gujarat
Definitions of technical terms used above:
  • Miniratna: A special status given by the Government to high-performing public sector companies.
  • Integrated Producer: A company that handles everything from raw material mining to making the final finished product.

Company Timeline & History

November 1967 Incorporated to take over from National Mineral Development Corporation Ltd.
March 1972 M/s Indian Copper Corporation Limited, Private Sector Company, located at Ghatsila, Jharkhand having facilities of Smelter and Refinery was Nationalized and made part of HCL.
February 1975 Fully integrated Copper complex from mining to refining came on stream at Khetri ( capacity 31,000 tonnes of refined copper)
November 1982 The largest hard rock open pit mine in the country came into stream at Malanjkhand in Madhya Pradesh with capacity 2 million tonnes ore
December 1989 Continuous Cast Wire Rod plant of South Wire Technology of capacity 60,000 MT was commissioned at Taloja in Maharashtra.
June 2015 Secondary Copper Smelter and Refinery Unit at Jhagadia, Gujarat ( 50,000 TPA of Copper Cathodes Conforming to LME-A grade ) was acquired

Mission, Vision and Values

The vision document broadly addressing the goals of the company is indicated below:

Vision

To strive to be a leading metal mining company and maximize total shareholder return by sustainably finding, developing, and mining copper ore and such other geologically associated minerals.

Mission

  • To achieve sustainable growth in business through optimum & efficient use of existing resources and assets.
  • To achieve rapid expansion of mining capacity through expansion of existing mines, re-opening of closed mines and green field projects.
  • Detailed exploration of existing mines and new mining leases to expand mining capacity.
  • To enhance the value of the Company by focusing on performance improvement.
  • To assimilate state-of-the art technology in exploration, mining and benefification of ores for competitive advantage.
  • To strive for continuous improvement in productivity and energy to bring at par with the best internationally.
  • To continue innovation through research & development.

Core Values

“Nurture the essence of Customer Relationship and Bonding.”
