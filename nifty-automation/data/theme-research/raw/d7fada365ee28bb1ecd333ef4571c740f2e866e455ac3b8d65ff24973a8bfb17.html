The Indian Hotels Company

The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) and its subsidiaries bring together a group of brands and businesses that offer a fusion of warm Indian hospitality and world-class service.

Share
Link

The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) and its subsidiaries bring together a group of brands and businesses that offer a fusion of warm Indian hospitality and world-class service.

These include Taj – the iconic brand for the most discerning travellers and ranked as World’s Strongest Hotel Brand 2025 and India’s Strongest Brand 2025 as per Brand Finance; Claridges Collection, a curated set of boutique luxury hotels merging elegance with historical charm; Brij, an experiential leisure offering; Atmantan, one of India’s leading integrated wellness destinations; SeleQtions, a named collection of hotels; Gateway, full-service hotels designed to be your gateway to exceptional destinations; Vivanta, sophisticated upscale hotels; Tree of Life, private escapes in tranquil settings and Ginger, which is revolutionising the lean luxe segment.

Incorporated by the founder of the Tata Group, Jamsetji Tata, the Company opened its first hotel – The Taj Mahal Palace, in Bombay in 1903. IHCL has a portfolio of 640 hotels including 263 in the pipeline globally across 4 continents, 15 countries and over 300 locations. The Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL) is India’s largest hospitality company by market capitalization. It is listed on the BSE and NSE.

IHC: Unlocking Tomorrow

Business Highlights

  • Expanded its portfolio to 630 hotels, powered by a record 250 signings and more than 130 hotel openings and onboardings, reinforcing its position as India's largest hospitality company.
  • Reinforced Taj's global standing after being recognised as the World's Strongest Hotel Brand 2025 for the fourth time by Brand Finance and as India's Strongest Brand across sectors.
  • Reached a portfolio spanning 15 countries and four continents, with an industry-leading development pipeline of 255 hotels.
  • Expanded its portfolio of brands:
    • Entered the integrated wellness segment through its strategic partnership with Atmantan Wellness Centre, one of India's best-known luxury wellness destinations.
    • Strengthened its experiential leisure portfolio through Brij Hospitality, expanding its presence in immersive and boutique travel experiences.
    • Expanded its luxury portfolio with the onboarding of Claridges Collection, a boutique luxury brand rooted in heritage and elegance.
  • Accelerated the growth of Ginger through its strategic partnership with ANK Hotels and Pride Hospitality, significantly expanding its footprint in the fast-growing midscale segment.
  • Continued the global expansion of Taj, with new signings including the brand's debut in Egypt and further growth in international markets.
  • Advanced its long-term growth strategy under Accelerate 2030, positioning the company for sustained expansion across brands, geographies and hospitality adjacencies.
  • Completed the migration of more than 120 hotels to Oracle Opera Cloud in just seven months, marking one of the largest technology transformation programmes undertaken by the company.  
  • Continued to deepen customer engagement through the growth of Taj InnerCircle – NeuPass and partnerships across the wider Tata ecosystem.
  • Sustained momentum on sustainability goals, sourcing 41% of its electricity from renewable energy while expanding water recycling and resource-efficiency initiatives across its portfolio.
  • Expanded EV charging infrastructure, waste reduction measures and responsible hospitality practices as part of its Paathya sustainability framework.
  • Advanced hospitality skilling and community-development initiatives, strengthening employability and career opportunities for young people across India.
