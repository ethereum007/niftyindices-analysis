About us

What we do?

UFlex is India’s largest flexible packaging company with headquarters in Noida, the National Capital Region of Delhi. With three manufacturing facilities, UFlex supplies to a majority of customers in India and also to multinational companies across the globe. With a strong presence in North America, Europe, Africa, South East Asia and the Middle East, UFlex has established itself as the largest flexible packaging manufacturer and exporter. UFlex has its manufacturing capacity of over 100,000 TPA with plants located in Noida and Jammu.

We offer an extensive product range in flexible packaging and laminated roll stocks. Some of the products of our portfolio include a wide variety of pouches comprising of 3D & 4D pouches with re-closable options, wicketed bags for baby and hygiene markets with handle, pet food pinch bottom bags, cement block bottom bags, Flexi-tubes, lids, confectionery foils, embossed foils, hygiene films, innolock pouches, pocket PTC zipper, spot embossing, electron beam curing, Cast ‘n’ Cure technologies etc. We also provide zip pouches that can be used to store food in refrigerators at home or carry food for lunch at school.Owing to full backward integration into films (BOPET, BOPP, CPP, Metalized Films), Chemicals (Inks, Coatings, Adhesives), Engineering (Converting & Packing machines), Holography (Films & Labels) and Cylinders (Electronic, Laser and Flexographic Plates), UFlex has an exclusive advantage to deliver customized solutions to its customers.

UFlex is equipped with four research and development laboratories approved by the Ministry of Industries, Government of India. With technologically advanced equipment such as differential scanning calorimeter, FTIR, OTR, MVTR, UTM, gas chromatograph, UFlex is efficiently capable of facilitating the analysis and development of modern flexible packaging solutions.

Pioneering in the Indian flexible packaging space, UFlex has introduced various innovative products that deliver consumer convenience and help brands offer extended shelf life to their products. At UFlex, the deep-rooted value is given utmost importance i.e. to offer consistent product quality which is carried out by maintaining systems and procedures as defined in ISO 9001:2000, BRC: IOP, ISO 22000.

Our marketing offices are located in India and overseas ensuring quick turnaround to our customers. All activities of the business complement with each other with the aim to deliver customer delight and satisfaction through meeting On-Time-In-Full (OTIF) and persistent quality.

Production Facility

UFlex operates with manufacturing facilities in Noida in Jammu with a total capacity of 135000 TPA. To ensure 'on-time-communication with stakeholders, all the plants and marketing offices are interlinked through an enterprise resource planning (ERP) system, which is operated with advanced oracle software.

Operational Excellence

Over the years, UFlex has channeled best practices across the business to achieve innovative results, higher productivity and reduction in consumption of raw materials at source. Focusing and following a legacy of decades, combined with technological up-gradation over time, UFlex enjoys one of the highest employee satisfaction indices in the industry. Owing to this, several people take great pride in being associated with UFlex and believe in greater responsibilities within the division and across the business.

Research & Development

Research & Development has been one of the edge of UFlex’ success over the years. We significantly invest in intricate research and development activities and high-tech equipment to ensure nothing less than advanced polymeric research and barrier technologies for an extended shelf life of products with its world-class packaging.

UFlex has pioneered the Electron Beam Coating Technology in India, known to only a few. We have successfully eliminated the print carrier layers. To add another feather to our hat, the advanced Electron Beam Curing is now offered with ‘Cast n Cure’ solutions to provide superior aesthetics to product packaging.
