About APSEZ

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone (APSEZ) is India’s largest and one of the world’s fastest-growing Integrated Transport Operators. With a network of 20 ports and terminals, including 16 strategically located ports in India and 4 international ports in Israel, Tanzania, Sri Lanka, and Australia, APSEZ plays a vital role in connecting businesses, markets, and global trade routes. Backed by an integrated logistics network that covers nearly 95% of India’s hinterland, APSEZ offers extensive connectivity that strengthens supply chains and supports trade across the country.

In India, APSEZ handles nearly 27% of the country’s cargo volumes, supported by a port capacity of 671 MMT, with a target of reaching 1 billion MT by 2030. Its world-class infrastructure manages a diverse range of cargo, including dry bulk, liquid bulk, crude, containers, and breakbulk, while accommodating some of the largest vessels operating in the region.
