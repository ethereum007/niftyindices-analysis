Three decades of wind technology evolution

Our wind turbine generators have evolved from early platforms to bigger, stronger machines that deliver more with each rotation. The perfect blend of global R&D and value engineering ensures our turbines offer you high returns across geographies.

Global in-house R&D. Three decades of wind intelligence

R&D ecosystem built to continuously improve turbine performance, reliability, and energy output for real-world conditions. Whether aerodynamics, blade design, drivetrain systems, low-carbon materials, or digital technologies.
