Inside Dixon’s Digital Evolution.

At Dixon, digital transformation is not just about adopting new technologies - it’s about building a smarter, more agile organisation from the ground up. Across functions, we are embedding intelligent systems, automating processes, and streamlining operations to enhance efficiency, transparency, and decision-making.


Here’s how our digital initiatives are shaping Dixon today:


Integrated Systems for Seamless Operations

  • Enterprise-wide implementation of ERP, CRM, and SCM platforms has enabled end-to-end process visibility and tighter integration across functions like sales, procurement, inventory, and finance.

  • Digitally connected manufacturing operations ensure greater accuracy and consistency through systems like manufacturing execution and production planning tools.

Smart Manufacturing with Industry 4.0

  • Advanced automation and IoT integration in our facilities provide real-time visibility into machine performance, line productivity, and shop-floor efficiency.

  • Data collected from machines is centralised and monitored via dashboards, enabling faster diagnostics, predictive maintenance, and proactive decision-making.

Data-Driven Decisions

  • We are building a strong data culture with centralised analytics systems that aggregate and process insights from across the value chain.

  • Business Intelligence tools help drive timely, data-backed decisions at every level — from daily operations to long-term planning.

Streamlined Product Development

  • Our new product development process is fully digitised, improving cross-functional collaboration and reducing time-to-market.

  • Traceability, documentation, and workflow management are now digitally tracked for better project governance.

Connected Warehousing and Logistics

  • Warehouses are now digitally mapped, integrated with barcode and scanning systems to track material flow, reduce errors, and improve dispatch timelines.

  • GPS-enabled logistics systems provide real-time visibility on vehicle movement and delivery tracking.

People and Processes, Upgraded

  • HR and administrative functions have been modernised with digital tools for attendance, payroll, performance reviews, recruitment, and more.

  • Workflow automation for finance, legal, procurement, and other support functions has reduced manual effort and turnaround time.

Improved Collaboration and Communication

  • Cloud-based tools support seamless cross-functional and remote collaboration, improving agility across all levels of the organisation.

  • Internal communication platforms ensure information is quickly and easily disseminated to the relevant teams.

The Road Ahead.

As we look to the future, our digital transformation roadmap is focused on scaling impact, deepening integration, and driving smarter decision-making across the organisation. Here’s how we’re building the next chapter of our digital journey:


Scaling digital tools across more facilities and production lines

We aim to extend the reach of our current digital systems - such as MES, digital dashboards, and automated tracking - across all our manufacturing units. This will ensure standardisation of best practices, real-time visibility across facilities, and a consistent quality of operations regardless of location.


Leveraging AI/ML for smart decision-making and anomaly detection

By incorporating artificial intelligence and machine learning into our systems, we plan to go beyond reactive and even predictive approaches. These technologies will help us uncover hidden patterns, detect anomalies in real-time, and make smarter, data-driven decisions to improve quality, uptime, and efficiency.


Investing in talent development through digital learning platforms

We believe people are at the heart of digital success. To support our teams, we’re creating digital learning ecosystems that provide on-demand training, upskilling resources, and personalised development paths. This ensures that our workforce is equipped to make the most of new tools and technologies.


Enhancing customer portals and product traceability with cloud-based tools

We’re working to make customer interactions more seamless and transparent. With cloud-enabled customer portals and digital traceability tools, our clients will have access to real-time updates, production status, compliance documentation, and complete visibility into their product lifecycle.


Integrating sustainability goals more closely with digital systems

Sustainability is a growing focus for us. We plan to deepen our integration of sustainability metrics into our digital platforms - tracking carbon footprint, energy efficiency, and material usage more granularly. This will help us make informed decisions and continuously improve our environmental performance.


Strengthening internal collaboration through unified data and communication platforms

To reduce silos and enable faster decision-making, we’re consolidating communication and data-sharing across departments. Unified digital platforms will allow smoother handoffs, shared dashboards, and better alignment between operations, planning, quality, and management teams.
