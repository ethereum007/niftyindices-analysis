Overview

Building circular value chains across lead, aluminium, plastics, lithium-ion batteries and tyre recycling. Delivering sustainable materials with reliable supply to industries worldwide.

Gravita India Limited is a global recycling company focused on building sustainable and efficient material recovery ecosystems. Established in 1992, Gravita has grown into a diversified, multi-material recycling group with operations across Asia, Africa, and Europe.

The Company operates advanced recycling and manufacturing facilities in India at Jaipur (Rajasthan), Kathua (Jammu & Kashmir), Mundra, and Chittoor (Andhra Pradesh), along with international plants in Romania, Ghana, Mozambique, Togo, Senegal, Tanzania, and Sri Lanka. Supported by a strong global procurement network, Gravita serves customers in more than 70 countries worldwide.

Gravita’s core business spans lead battery recycling, aluminium alloy manufacturing, plastic recycling, and waste tyre recycling. Its product portfolio includes specialized lead alloys, red lead, lead oxide, aluminium alloys, plastic granules, recovered carbon black, and Tyre Pyrolysis Oil (TPO). The Company has also executed more than 70 turnkey recycling projects globally, offering end-to-end solutions from design and engineering to commissioning.

As part of its strategic expansion, Gravita has launched a dedicated Lithium-ion Battery Recycling vertical, with a recycling plant established at Mundra, Gujarat. The Company has also announced its entry into the copper recycling segment and signed a binding agreement to acquire a stake in Rashtriya Metal Industries Limited, strengthening its presence in non-ferrous metals.

Listed on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) Ltd., Gravita remains committed to responsible growth, operational excellence, and advancing the global circular economy.

Mission

To be amongst the top 5 global companies in recycling.

Our company's mission is centred around four core pillars that guide our business strategy: diversification, sustainable growth, eco-friendly tech innovation, and value creation for all stakeholders.

Diversification a crucial component of our mission. We firmly believe that diversification is essential for crafting robust and resilient strategies that combines risk mitigation with opportunity exploration.

Sustainable growth is an enduring value that not only safeguards the environment but also has a positive impact on society.

Eco-friendly tech innovationis a core focus area for our company. We are committed to harnessing the power of technology for the betterment of the environment and society.

Value creation for all stakeholders is a central tenet of our mission. We firmly believe that the well-being of all is crucial for long-term success and aligned with the principles of corporate social responsibility.

Vision

To be the most valuable company in the recycling space globally.

At Gravita, we are quality-conscious and committed to providing our customers with the highest service standards.

Values

Our sense of purpose is driven by a strong focus on fairness, trust, respect, passion, and nurturing relationships.

Fairness is a key to our business practices. We believe in conducting our operations fairly and ethically, ensuring all stakeholders are treated equitably.

Trust is the foundation of our relationships with our stakeholders. We value the trust that our stakeholders have placed in us and work tirelessly to maintain and strengthen it.

Respect is an essential component of our culture. We treat all our stakeholders equally, regardless of their background, position, or status.

Passion is what drives us to succeed. We are passionate about recycling and creating a sustainable future for generations.

Nurturing relationships is one of our core values. We foster long-term relationships with our stakeholders based on mutual trust, respect, and collaboration.
