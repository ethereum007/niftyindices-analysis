About Us

Ircon International Limited (IRCON), a government company incorporated by the Central Government (Ministry of Railways) under the Companies Act, 1956 on 28th April, 1976 originally under the name Indian Railway Construction Company Limited, is the leading turnkey construction company in the public sector known for its quality, commitment and consistency in terms of Performance.

Our Vision

To be recognized nationally and internationally as a construction organization comparable with the best in the field covering the entire spectrum...

Our Mission

Values

CMD's Message

IRCON has emerged as a leading enterprise in India in the field of `Transport Infrastructure’. Facing the new challenges and opportunities, IRCON is determined to enhance its business of construction and infrastructure services. We shall promote the technology and management innovation as well as boost the overall development of the Company in a coordinated and sustainable manner.

Shri Saleem Ahmad

Chairman & Managing Director (Additional Charge)

Public Advisory: Beware of Fake Recruitment Websites

IRCON International Limited publishes all career notices, vacancies and recruitment updates only on its official website, www.ircon.org.

It has come to our notice that fraudulent websites are impersonating IRCON, posting fake vacancies and asking candidates to register. One such fake website is:

This website is not owned by or associated with IRCON. Do not register, share personal documents, or make any payment on it or on any similar website.
