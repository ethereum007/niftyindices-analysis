DCX

Capabilities

Our Strength

Bringing Excellence Everyday…

DCX Systems is a trusted engineering and manufacturing partner for global aerospace and defense companies. We specialize in the manufacturing of complex electronic systems, sub-systems, RF and microwave modules, radars, cable & wire harness assemblies, and electro-mechanical solutions for mission-critical platforms.

Our vertically integrated capabilities enable us to support customers across the entire product lifecycle from product development, prototyping to subsystem manufacturing, system integration to testing, qualification, final delivery and lifecycle support. This integrated approach ensures faster turnaround times, cost-efficient production, and sustained manufacturability.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, advanced testing facilities, and highly skilled engineering teams, DCX delivers reliable and high-performance solutions that meet stringent aerospace and defense standards. Our expertise in system integration, electronic assemblies, and environmental testing supports programs across land, naval, and airborne platforms.

Through strong partnerships with global OEMs and adherence to international quality standards, DCX consistently delivers dependable, scalable, and mission-ready solutions to its customers.

End-to-End Capabilities

Both DCX & Raneal have Obtained Defense industrial license of Category-A for manufacturing the Secret and Highly Classified Systems and modules which includes:

  • RADAR and EW Systems and Sub-Systems
  • AVIONICS & Defence Electronic Equipment
  • Microwave Sub-Modules for Command and Guidance units for Missile Sub Systems
  • MRO for above Systems

MRO Services

Strategically located in Aerospace Special Economic Zone with an advanced and Modern Manufacturing Facility

In 2020, DCX commissioned a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility at the Hi-Tech Defence and Aerospace Park SEZ, Bengaluru, Karnataka, strengthening its aerospace and defence manufacturing capabilities.

The facility is situated within a SEZ that offers duty free imports, exemption from GST and supplies that are zero rated under extant regulations.

Strategically positioned near key domestic customers, DCX ensures efficient program execution and accelerated delivery cycles.

Our capabilities include system integration, Sub-systems manufacturing, Box-builds, PCB assembly, cable and wire harness assemblies along with prototype manufacturing and testing of complex defence and aerospace platforms with high reliability and quality assurance. Our Set up caters for end-to-end in-house environmental and electrical testing, with state-of-the-art inspection and testing infrastructure.

The facility features advanced infrastructure and process capabilities, ensuring precision manufacturing, operational efficiency, and compliance with demanding defence and aerospace standards.
