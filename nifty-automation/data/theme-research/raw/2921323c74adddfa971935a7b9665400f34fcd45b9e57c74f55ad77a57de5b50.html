Railways

KEC is an integrated player in the Railway EPC industry, providing complete turnkey solutions for all types of railway contracts.

  • Overhead Electrification
  • Signalling & Telecommunication for outdoor and indoor installation including Electronic Interlocking (EI) for new lines and capacity augmentation works
  • Traction substations
  • Doubling & Tripling of tracks and New Railway Lines
  • Regional Rapid Transit System
  • Civil Infrastructure – Road Over Bridge, Bridges, Tunnels, Stations & Platforms

The Company has integrated backwards to manufacture contact & catenary conductors, signalling cables as well as galvanised steel structures for railways. It has the capacity to manufacture 48,000 MTPA of railway structures.

One of the early entrants in the railway electrification space, KEC has electrified nearly 40% of the Indian Railway Network till date, spanning more than 17,000 km.

The Company continues to enhance its capabilities to cater to the evolving needs of the railway industry by fast-tracking project execution as well as adopting new technologies & mechanised construction methods that enable it to meet the demands of a progressing nation. The Company also undertakes Railway Infrastructure jobs for private customers and has developed competencies and capabilities to undertake Railway projects including composite jobs in any challenging terrain or environment. KEC has successfully diversified its client portfolio to include all the major players in the railway industry - CORE, RVNL, IRCON, RITES and PGCIL.

In September 2010, KEC acquired Jay Signaling Private Limited, an Indian railway signalling automation systems and technology company. The acquisition strengthened its capability for signalling works and thereby increased its service offering in railway projects across the globe.
