About Us

Saregama India Ltd., part of the prestigious RPSG Group, is India's leading Entertainment IP company with a legacy dating back to 1902. Its diverse portfolio includes film music and artiste driven music, artiste and influencer management, live events, films, digital series, television series, and retail products. The company is publicly listed on the NSE and BSE. The company has shown a growth in Topline at 17% CAGR and Operational PBT at 14% CAGR over last 5 years.

Saregama's business spans across three core segments: Content Creation (music, films, TV/digital series, and short-format content), Live Events (Music, Devotional, Experiential, and Comedy properties), and Artiste Management (Music and Influencers).

With an expansive library of 180,000+ songs, 70+ films, 55+ digital series, and 10,000+ hours of TV content, Saregama continues to reinforce its stature as a leading powerhouse in the entertainment sector.

It's strategic investment in Bhansali Productions provides exclusive access to marquee Hindi film music at a predictable cost structure, strengthening Saregama's future content pipeline while leveraging the complementary strengths of both partners.

Saregama boasts a robust digital presence with over 650 million subscribers and followers across YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook. Through its acquisition of Pocket Aces—known for FilterCopy, Dice Media, along with influencer marketing capabilities through its subsidiary Finnet Media Limited, and an impressive roaster of 300+ influencers—Saregama broadened its digital presence by pairing its music library with Pocket Aces' content and distribution strengths to better reach digital-native audiences.
