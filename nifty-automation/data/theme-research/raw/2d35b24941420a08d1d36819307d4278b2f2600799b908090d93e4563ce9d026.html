Our Port Businesses

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited (APSEZ Ltd.) is India’s largest and the world’s fastest‑growing integrated transport utility. The company operates 19 ports and terminals, including 15 in India and 4 international ports across Australia, Colombo, Israel, and Tanzania—creating one of the most extensive and strategically placed port networks in the region.
