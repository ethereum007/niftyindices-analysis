Pilot Plant: Bridging Lab & Plant

Nurturing Seeds of Innovation

The development work of new products and processes is well supported by state-of-the-art DCS-operated Pilot Plant. The plant is multi-purpose in nature and can handle Process Development work involving sub atmospheric to high pressure (up to 50 kg/cm 2 ) conditions over a wide range of temperature (-80C to >350C). The plant equipment includes a variety of Reactors, Distillation Columns, Heat Exchangers and related paraphernalia in various materials of construction.

BUILT FOR SCALE. DESIGNED FOR TRUST.

SRF’s Specialty Chemicals infrastructure spans over 20 advanced manufacturing units located across its integrated complexes in Bhiwadi (Rajasthan) and Dahej (Gujarat). These multi-purpose, flexible facilities enable end-to-end execution – from product design to large-scale despatch – offering a decisive competitive edge.
