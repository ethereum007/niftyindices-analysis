Our Vision

Seqrite envisions a world where every business is digitally safer and more secure than the last. We are dedicated to advancing cybersecurity to ensure a protected digital environment for all.

Who We Are?

Seqrite is the enterprise arm of Quick Heal Technologies Limited, India’s first listed cybersecurity company. With a rich heritage of innovation, we are the first and only Indian company to collaborate with the US Government on critical cybersecurity projects.

What We Do?

Seqrite provides cutting-edge cybersecurity solutions and services designed to safeguard businesses from evolving threats. Leveraging our patented AI/ML-powered technology stack, we deliver comprehensive protection across devices, applications, networks, cloud, data, and identity.

Our solutions stand out for their ease of deployment and seamless integration, offering unparalleled defense against emerging and sophisticated threats. Powered by state-of-the-art threat intelligence and expertly curated playbooks, our offerings are backed by world-class service delivered by top-tier security professionals at Seqrite Labs one of the largest malware analysis lab.

Seqrite is one of the full-stack cybersecurity company aligned with Cybersecurity Mesh Architecture (CSMA) recommendations, ensuring businesses stay resilient in the face of modern cyber risks.

Dr. Kailash Katkar

Chairman and Managing Director

Dr. Kailash Katkar is the Chairman and Managing Director of Quick Heal Technologies Limited, India’s leading IT Security Solutions Company. He is responsible for the overall leadership, strategy and management of the company. Under his leadership, Quick Heal became the first Indian software product company to hit the capital markets in 2016.

Born in 1966 to a Maharashtrian family at Rahimatpur, a small village near Satara in Maharashtra, Kailash grew up in Pune and from a very young age, chose to break the norms of attending rigorous classroom sessions and decided to divert his inquisitiveness and hard work to start his own business. This little spur of thought guided by sheer grit and gumption drove him to set up his own business – from a small one-room, boot-strapped electrical device repair station to an IT Security Solutions empire spread worldwide.

As a true visionary, his strategic business approach has continuously produced significant business value for Quick Heal in the form of financial performance, customer satisfaction, market share and productivity. His strong commitment to operational excellence, an innovative approach to business problems, and an aptitude for partnering cross-functionally has made Quick Heal the most preferred consumer security solutions brand. In 2015, the company successfully forayed into enterprise cybersecurity with the launch of the brand ‘Seqrite’.

Recently, Quick Heal transformed the consumer cybersecurity in a whole new category by launching India’s First Fraud Prevention Solution AntiFraud.AI. His brainchild, Quick Heal Foundation, functions in the areas of community welfare, education and creating awareness around cybersecurity. The foundation has provided training and guidance on cybersecurity to millions of students. In 2017, Chitkara University felicitated Kailash with the Honorary Doctorate for his outstanding contribution to Business, Education and Humanity.

Dr. Sanjay Katkar

Joint Managing Director

Dr. Sanjay Katkar co-founded Quick Heal Technologies Limited in the year 1995. He is the Joint Managing Director and an executive member of the Board of Directors.

n his role, he is leading the global technology strategy and is responsible for the core research and development of the products, the malware research lab, and driving product vision and innovation to continuously improve cybersecurity.

Sanjay initiated the antivirus product development and, over the years, built all the teams, including threat research, product development, quality assurance, and product management. With a highly distinctive personality and a passion for technology, Sanjay created the indigenously designed and developed antivirus. His significant and outstanding contribution has earned Quick Heal a global position.

In recognition of his lifelong contribution to the cybersecurity ecosystem, Dr. Sanjay Katkar was honored with the Lifetime Achievement Award (Cyber) at the Surakshit Pune Awards 2025.

A distinguished thought leader, Sanjay is a sought-after speaker at prominent industry forums and has addressed audiences at leading institutions, including IIMs and IITs. He has also served as the Director of the Association of Anti-Virus Researchers, Asia. He enlightens the industry at various DSCI and NASSCOM events.

Sanjay holds a Master’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Pune. In 2019, MIT-ADT University, Pune, felicitated Sanjay Katkar with an Honorary Doctorate for his outstanding contribution in the field of Cybersecurity.
