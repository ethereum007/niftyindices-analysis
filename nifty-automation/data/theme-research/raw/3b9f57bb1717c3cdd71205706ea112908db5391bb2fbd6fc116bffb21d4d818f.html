Contract Logistics & Multimodal Transport Solutions

Our overarching goal is to optimise the journey of all inputs and finished products, ensuring that the entire supply chain process is efficient, reliable, and customer-centric. We strive to provide a seamless experience for our customers, from the origin of goods to the final delivery to the end customer, with the ultimate aim of enhancing customer satisfaction. We constantly seek ways to streamline processes, eliminate inefficiencies, and reduce TAT at every stage of the supply chain. This includes efficient handling, storage and delivery of goods, utilising cutting-edge technologies. By prioritising efficiency, reliability, and customer satisfaction, we aim to become a trusted partner for our customers, delivering value-added services and solutions that ignite success.

Integrated Warehousing & Distribution

Our tailor-made integrated warehousing and distribution solutions deliver greater efficiency, agility, flexibility and robustness to supply chains of our clients. Enhancing these solutions with our technology, helps to bring transparency to the operations, enabling continuous optimisation, making the journey from supplier to factories and factories to customers more efficient. An extensive network of strategically located multi-client warehouses, enables us to reach to a larger population and provides flexibility to our client operations. Coupled with an extensive transportation network, we enable agile distribution and consolidation for our clients. We have built long-standing relationships with business partners that provide work assets and help us maintain scalability and flexibility while meeting the diverse needs of customers across multiple industries and geographies.

Transport Desk Management

We offer Transport Desk Management as a value-added service to our customers, enabling them to streamline their transportation operations and reduce costs. Through Transport Desk Management, we take over all transportation operations, serving as a single point of contact operationally and contractually for the customer. This enables our customers to focus on their core business activities while leaving the transportation operations to us. Our expertise and experience in optimising vehicle placement and delivery schedules results in consistent cost savings for our customers. Our data analytics gives our customers greater visibility and control over their transportation operations, enabling them to take better business decisions.

Full Truck Load Transportation

Our large fleet of dedicated vehicles cater to any transportation requirement across India. With more than 15,000 vehicles plying every month, we have the resources and expertise to provide a full suite of transportation solutions to our customers, paired with a wide variety of experience across industries. Our fleet of dedicated vehicles includes trucks, trailers, and other specialised vehicles designed to meet the specific needs of our customers. Our technology infrastructure includes advanced tracking and reporting tools, as well as customised reporting and analytics, providing our customers with actionable insights into their transportation operations and identifying areas for improvement.

Auto Outbound

Our Auto Outbound activities include a range of critical functions that ensure smooth and efficient outbound operations. They are designed to maintain high quality standards, with a special focus on pre-outbound quality check. Custody transfer involves the transfer of goods from our warehouse to the transportation vehicle. We use advanced scheduling tools and techniques for planning and optimising our operations. A thorough condition check is conducted before trailer loading to ensure that it is in good working order and ready for transportation. Our Auto Outbound services are designed to translate sales plan into a dispatch plan through the use of advanced dispatch optimiser and vehicle design capability.

Rail Logistics

We are registered with railways for cargo movement, which allows us to provide our customers with seamless end-to-end (E2E) operations for the automotive sector. Our commitment to excellence in service has enabled us to reduce transit time, increase efficiency, and drive cost-effectiveness for our valued customers. With our 24×7 operations, we ensure that our customers’ needs are met round-the-clock, providing reliable and timely service. Our escort facility coupled with real-time tracking ensures the safety and security of our shipments. Increased placements enable us to transport more goods at a time reducing the overall cost of transportation.

Fulfilment Centres

Our fulfillment centres have a strong primary, secondary, and tertiary distribution network that enables us to deliver products quickly and efficiently to our customers, resulting in increased customer satisfaction and loyalty. With our dedicated control tower, we efficiently manage our operations and inventory to ensure that our customers receive the right products at the right time. Our sort centres and hubs located in various cities further improve our distribution network, resulting in faster delivery times and reduced shipping costs. We operate 365 days a year and offer COD management, ensuring that our customers have access to their products whenever they need them. We also provide on-time-in-full inventory and reliable inventory management to prevent stockouts and deliver orders on time. Our flexible order fulfilment options cater to the diverse needs of our customers, and our electric last mile delivery option provides an eco-friendly and cost-effective solution for delivering products to their doorsteps.

Sort Centres

Our sort centres enable us to sort and organise shipments by destination, reducing transit times and improving delivery accuracy. Additionally, we offer dedicated part truck load dispatches, which ensure that shipments are transported directly from our facility to their destination. We also deploy advanced transportation management systems (TMS) and warehouse management systems (WMS) to track and manage shipments in real time, ensuring that they are delivered on time and in excellent condition.

Distribution Solutions

Our strong pan-India presence provides us with the expertise and experience needed to serve a wide range of industries and customers. Our extensive network of partners and collaborators enables us to meet the distribution needs of our clients effectively, ensuring that their products are delivered quickly and efficiently to their customers. To further enhance our distribution capabilities, we leverage expert insights on route topography and delivery location entry points. This enables us to plan delivery routes more effectively, reducing transit times and improving delivery accuracy. By understanding the topography of different regions, we can identify the most efficient routes for delivery, taking into account factors such as terrain, traffic conditions, and local regulations.

Warehousing Solutions

Our warehousing solutions primarily include inventory control, storage management and value-added services. We manage over 22 million square feet of warehousing space at multiple locations across the country. These include a mix of built-to-suit, dedicated, carbon neutral and multi-user warehouses. We have deployed customised Material Handling Equipment (MHE) as well as extensive automation. We have multi-brand large stockyards located strategically across the country, ensuring flexibility and scalability. We use best-in-class latest proprietary tools & technologies for improving productivity and increasing efficiency. We understand that every business has different requirements. And to fulfil every need, we have designed a full suite of solutions and value-add services – sourcing & procurement, shop floor management, customised reporting, order management, inventory control, material supply management, kitting, sequencing and line-side delivery, bundling & unbundling, light/sub-assembly, packaging, re-packaging, re-boxing, labelling, sorting, audit support, reverse logistics and returns management. In addition to warehousing, we offer a host of services such as locating the perfect site for your distribution centre, designing a layout & set up processes, material handling equipment management, maintenance of warehouse and MHE, safety management, housekeeping, security, and support staff management.

In-Plant Logistics

Managing the internal logistics of factories is as important as managing the production processes to improve efficiency.

Our in-plant logistics services are customised services, which are aligned to the customers’ production schedule. We play an important role in optimising in-plant operations for our customers.

We supply real-time data of all kinds required for planning improvements in efficiency and precision in operations by means of technology incorporated in our in-plant logistics systems for factories. Through our Stores and Line Feeding (SLF) process, we provide services like kit creation and building, trolley loading, high-value parts management, receipt and physical verification of materials, just in time (JIT) and just in sequence (JIS) operations
